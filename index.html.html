About Us

Welcome to Plus Enterprises, your trusted partner in agricultural nutrition.

At Plus Enterprises, we supply high-quality agricultural inputs that help farmers grow healthier crops, improve soil fertility and get better results from every season.

We specialize in the import and supply of Amino Acid 80% Powder and Fulvic Acid 70% Powder, carefully selected to meet the nutritional needs of modern agriculture.

Our mission is to make quality crop nutrition easy to reach for farmers, growers and agricultural businesses. Better nutrition builds stronger plants, higher productivity and a more sustainable future for farming.

We focus on product quality, honest service and long-term relationships with our customers.
